## What the extension does

Web Saver saves a file you can already see or open on a page:

- Right-click an image, video, audio item, or file link, or scan the page and pick files.
- Choose Dropbox, Google Drive, or both, and a folder in that account.
- The file is uploaded to the account you connected.

Web Saver does not host your files or keep a copy. A public file may be fetched by Dropbox from its address. Other files are downloaded in your browser, using the login you already have on that site, and then uploaded. What is stored and sent is described in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Your accounts

You use your own Dropbox account, Google account, or both. Connecting asks that service for permission to list and create folders and to save the files you choose. The permissions requested are listed in the privacy policy.

You can disconnect from the settings page at any time, and you can revoke Web Saver from your Dropbox or Google account. Disconnecting does not delete files already saved.

By default, Dropbox sign-in uses Web Saver's public Dropbox app, which only identifies the extension to Dropbox. You can instead connect with a Dropbox app you create. That app is yours. You follow Dropbox's rules for it, and only you can manage or delete it.

Google Drive sign-in uses the Google OAuth client shipped with the extension. Google's terms apply to your Google account and to files in Drive.

## Files you save

You decide what to save. Save only files you have the right to copy, including the rules of the website, copyright, and the terms of any account you are signed into.

Saving uses your normal browser session on that site, the same way a download you start yourself would. Web Saver does not open anything you cannot already open.

Files you save are stored by Dropbox or Google in the folder you pick. Those companies' terms cover the files once they are in your account. Web Saver cannot read files already in your Dropbox, and it does not browse or download files already in your Drive.

- **Dropbox**, under the [Dropbox Terms of Service](https://www.dropbox.com/terms)
